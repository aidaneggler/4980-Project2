GAME CONCEPT

I wanted to make a first person game that explores using different wavelengths of lights to solve a puzzle. You have to explore the environment and discover
clues that can only be seen with certain lights. The goal is to find the information needed to solve the puzzles and escape.

CORE MECHANIC

The main mechanic is switching between different wavelengths of light. The main mechanic is switching between different wavelengths of light.
The player starts with a white flashlight which helps them see and explore the room. Other types of flashlights can be collected as they progress. 
A UV flashlight reveals hidden writing, while a red flashlight reveals other hidden objects, such as a switch. These objects only appear when the 
correct light is shining directly on them. Turning away, switching wavelengths, and turning the flashlight off hides them again.

GAMEPLAY LOOP

The player explores a dark room using a flashlight, finds a new one, uses it to reveal clues and applies those clues to solve a puzzle. Solving the puzzle 
shows a new part of the room or unlocks a new flashlight. This continues until the player can escape the room. In this prototype, the player collects the
UV light and uses it to find the hidden code. Then they put the code into the keypad which unlocks the cabinet on the wall. From there the player gets
the red flashlight and then they use that to find the switch that opens the exit door. The player wins by leaving the room. 

The light sources are not just tools for visibility. They determine what information the player is able to discover and interact with.
The same room can contain different clues depending on the wavelength being used so the player has to experiment with their flashlights and 
think about where to aim them. Instead ofcollecting keys or following a visible path, the player uses light to uncover the puzzle itself.

The idea is to make players curious about their surroundings and give them the satisfaction of discovering something that was hidden in 
plain sight. The first prototype tests whether switching wavelengths and revealing clues is understandable, interesting, and fun enough to 
support an escape-room game.

CONTROLS

WASD - Move
Mouse - Look around and aim the flashlight
F - Turn flashlight on or off
1 - White light
2 - UV light (after collecting it)
3 - Red light (after collecting it)
E - Interact with objects and keypads; restart after escaping
Number keys - Enter keypad code while using the keypad
Enter - Submit keypad code
Backspace - Delete a keypad digit

PROTOTYPE SCOPE & PLAYTESTING

The prototype focuses on one room with three light types, two linked puzzles, and a win/restart loop. The environment 
and objects are simple because the priority is testing the mechanic. During playtesting I want 
to see whether players discover the flashlights and clues without being told where they are, understand how to use each 
wavelength, and enjoy figuring out the escape sequence. I'll use player observations and feedback to improve clarity, 
puzzle difficulty, and the light reveal mechanic.
